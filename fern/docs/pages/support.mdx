## 研发阶段 / Development stage

BinFlow 尚在 P0，不提供已发布产品、SDK、生产 SLA 或已验证的全兼容声明。
BinFlow is in P0 and does not yet offer a released product/SDK, production SLA or verified full-parity claim.

公开问题通过 [产品仓库](https://github.com/0ldlight/bf) 提交；文档问题通过 [文档仓库](https://github.com/0ldlight/binflow-docs) 反馈。研发派单与审批使用现有 [Linear 项目](https://linear.app/binfloow/project/binflow-2ad2bc04f63e)。
Use the relevant source/docs repository for public issues and the existing Linear project for engineering tasks/approvals.

提交可复现步骤、提交/制品摘要、期望与实际行为，以及必要的脱敏日志。不要上传密码、Token、私钥、完整 kubeconfig、原版安装包或反编译源码。
Provide reproducible steps, identities, expectations and sanitized evidence; exclude secrets and controlled research materials.
